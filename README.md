# Cryo-ET and Subtomogram averaging Workshop

[![Licence](https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-CC_BY_4.0-blue)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The focus of the workshop is to provide training in some of the most recent developments of CryoET and STA data processing using for instance **nextPYP**, **TomoBEAR**, **Warp/M**, taught by the developers themselves. The workshop provides hands-on training in CryoET and STA encompassing all stages of the pipeline.

<!-- generated:learning-outcomes -->
## Learning outcomes

After completing this training, participants will be able to:

- Alignment and reconstruction of tilt series
- Manual and automated particle picking
- Archiving and cataloguing of tilt series, tomograms and annotations
- Creation of subtomogram averaging projects
- Advanced tools in subtomogram averaging
- Classification methods
- Creation of 3D maps
